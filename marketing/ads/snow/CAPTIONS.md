# Snowflake Projector Lamp: hooks, captions, hashtags

## Opening hooks (all rendered, 3 s each: marketing/reels/hooks/)
- **A (chosen):** "WHY IS IT SNOWING INSIDE?!" + kicker "wait... look at the ceiling!"
  A question the viewer can't answer yet, only 4 big words, readable in ~0.5 s, and it names the absurd
  thing on screen. Strongest curiosity gap.
- **B:** "THIS LIGHT CHANGES THE WHOLE ROOM." A statement, so it gives the payoff away and reads more like an ad.
- **C:** "IMAGINE WAKING UP TO THIS..." Opens on the ceiling view. Dreamy, but "imagine" asks for effort and is slower to read.
